# Morrowgear Airborne Solar Service Dock v2

## Approval status

This directory is a visual proposal only. Do not replace the in-game assets until the rendered comparisons are approved.

## Coordinate system and proportions

- World `+Z`: up.
- World `+Y`: visual front and the first service emitter.
- Overall station diameter: `15.6` Blender units (`1.00`).
- Overall structural height: `2.25` units (`0.144`).
- Central machinery diameter: `6.10` units (`0.391`).
- Each solar wing spans `87 degrees`, leaving three functional notches.
- Three service emitters and three nested relay cradles are spaced at exactly `120 degrees`.

## Recognition features

1. Three independent fan-shaped solar wings, not a continuous decorated disc.
2. A dense, stepped central reactor with a recessed cyan X aperture.
3. Three radial cylindrical service emitters occupying the gaps between solar wings.
4. Layered segmented perimeter armor with visible fasteners and panel breaks.
5. A mechanically complete underside with three power trunks, vents, and a central induction coil.
6. Thick charging relays with a faceted armored shell and a recessed ventral emitter.

## Components and state

| Component | Quantity | State | Emissive behavior |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Solar wing | 3 | Fixed | Thin cyan bus lines only |
| Central reactor rings | 5 | Fixed | Inner rings and X aperture |
| Service emitter | 3 | Fixed | Cyan coil rings and nozzle |
| Relay cradle | 3 | Fixed | Bay locator strips |

## Material hierarchy

- Primary armor: near-black gunmetal, medium roughness.
- Secondary mechanisms: dark neutral steel with brighter worn edges.
- Solar cells: blue-black, low roughness, fine grid separation.
- Energy surfaces: cyan, recessed rather than attached as luminous rods.
- Maintenance markings: restrained amber, used only for latches and warnings.

## Detail hierarchy

- Long range: three solar wings, three emitter notches, and the central reactor silhouette.
- Medium range: segmented perimeter armor, cell frames, radial power buses, emitter coil bands, and relay shell leaves.
- Close range: panel seams, collector traces, junction boxes, hinges, clamps, cooling banks, vents, fasteners, and warning latches.
- Emission is intentionally restricted to the reactor, power buses, emitter coils, and relay apertures. Solar-cell subdivision is primarily metallic and must not read as decorative neon.
